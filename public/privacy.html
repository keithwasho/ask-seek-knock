Privacy

Last updated: October 26th, 2026 · Back to Ask, Seek, and Knock

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in.
  • A record of each session: the date and time, and the cost of producing the answer. This is used to run free trials and keep the service sustainable.
  • Subscription details from Stripe: your plan, renewal date, and payment status. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Contact form messages: your name, email address, and message, so we can reply. We also record the internet address a message was sent from, only to prevent spam.

What we do not keep

We do not save what you write or the answers you receive. Your question is sent to our AI provider only to write your answer. While you are signing in, an unsent draft is kept in your own browser on your device so you do not lose it.

Who processes your information

  • Anthropic writes the answers. Your question is sent to Anthropic to produce your answer, under Anthropic's commercial terms.
  • Supabase handles sign-in and stores your email and session records.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails.
  • Google measures, using cookies, whether visitors arrived from our Google ads and went on to sign up or subscribe, so we know which ads help people find us. It does not receive your questions or answers. You can manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Your choices

To get a copy of your information or delete your account, contact us through our contact form. Cancelling a subscription is done from "Manage subscription" on the site.

Children

The service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date above.
